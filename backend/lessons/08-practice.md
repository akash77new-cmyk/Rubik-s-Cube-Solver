# Getting faster: practice tips

How to improve once you can solve the cube.

## Learn the method first

Solve the cube many times with the beginner method until you no longer need notes. Speed comes
from not having to think about what to do next, not from turning faster.

## Finger tricks

Turn the right and top layers with your fingers instead of your whole hand: push the top layer
with your index finger for U moves, and turn the right side with your wrist for R moves. Most
fast algorithms use R and U moves for this reason.

## Look ahead

While doing one step, start looking for the pieces of the next step. Turning a little slower but
without pauses is faster overall than turning fast and stopping to search.

## Look after your cube

A cube that turns smoothly is easier to solve. If it feels stiff, it may need a little cube
lubricant or a tension adjustment. Keep it clean and dry.

## What to learn next

After the beginner method, many people learn CFOP: the cross, then the first two layers
together (F2L), then the last layer with more algorithms (OLL and PLL). The app's Analytics page
shows your progress over time.
