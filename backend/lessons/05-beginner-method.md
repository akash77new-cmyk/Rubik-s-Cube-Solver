# The beginner method, layer by layer

A method a person can learn: solve the cube in seven stages, white layer first.

## The idea

The beginner method solves the cube in layers: first the white layer, then the middle layer,
then the yellow layer, in seven stages. Each stage uses a few short move sequences, called
algorithms, that move the pieces you want and bring the solved parts back. Do stages 1 and 2 with
white on top. Then turn the cube over, keeping the same colour facing you, and do stages 3 to 7
with yellow on top. The Learn page guides you through every stage on your own cube.

## Stage 1: the white cross

With white on top, bring the four white edges next to the white centre, so that each edge's other
colour also matches the centre of its side. This stage needs no algorithm, just looking and
thinking. The easiest case is an edge in the bottom layer with white facing down: turn the bottom
until it is under its centre, then turn that side twice. An edge with white facing out needs a
side turn instead; for example, an edge at the bottom right with white facing right goes to the
top front with R F' R'.

## Stage 2: the white corners

Keep white on top. Find a white corner in the bottom layer and turn the bottom until the corner
is right under its place. If that place is at the front right, repeat R' D' R D, the corner
trick, until the corner is solved: 1, 3 or 5 times. The white cross comes back each time. At
other places the same trick is done at that corner, and the app shows its moves. A white corner
stuck in the top layer comes down with one corner trick at its place.

## Stage 3: the middle layer

Turn the cube over: yellow on top, the same colour facing you. Find an edge in the top layer
without yellow. Turn the top until its side colour sits above the matching centre. If its top
colour matches the centre on the right, do U R U' R' U' F' U F. If it matches the left, do
U' L' U L U F U' F'. An edge stuck in the middle layer in the wrong place comes out with either
algorithm done at its place.

## Stage 4: the yellow cross

Look at the yellow stickers on top. Turn the top so that an L shape is at the back left, or a
line goes from left to right, and do F R U R' U' F'. A dot (only the centre is yellow) needs the
algorithm more than once. Repeat until you see the yellow cross.

## Stage 5: the yellow face

Count the yellow corners facing up. With one, turn the top so it is at the front left. With
none, turn until the front-left corner's yellow sticker faces left; with two, until it faces you.
Then do the Sune: R U R' U R U2 R'. Repeat until the whole top is yellow, at most three times.

## Stage 6: the yellow corners

Now put the yellow corners in their places. Look for headlights: two top corners on one side
whose side stickers have the same colour. Turn the top so the headlights are at the back and do
R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2. With no headlights, do it from anywhere first. When the corners are in
the right order, turn the top until they match the centres.

## Stage 7: the yellow edges

Find a side whose top edge already matches its centre. Turn the top so that side is at the back,
do the edge cycle R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2, then turn the top back. If the edges went round the
wrong way, do it again. Do not panic when the cube looks scrambled in the middle of an
algorithm: the solved parts come back when it ends.
