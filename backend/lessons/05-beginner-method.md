# The beginner method, layer by layer

A method a person can learn: solve the cube one layer at a time, white side first.

## The idea

The beginner method solves the cube in layers: first the white layer, then the middle layer,
then the yellow layer. Each stage uses a few short move sequences, called algorithms, that
change only the pieces you want while the solved parts come back in place.

## Step 1: the white cross

Put the white centre on top and bring the four white edges next to it, so that each edge's other
colour also matches the centre of its side. This step is done by looking and thinking, without
algorithms. Beginners take a while here; it gets fast with practice.

## Step 2: the white corners

Keep the white cross on top. Find a white corner in the bottom layer and turn the bottom until
the corner is right under its place, at the front right. Repeat R' D' R D until the corner is
solved (2, 4 or 6 times). Do this for all four corners, then turn the cube over so white is at
the bottom.

## Step 3: the middle layer

Find an edge in the top layer without yellow. Line its front colour up with its centre. To send
it to the right, do U R U' R' U' F' U F. To send it to the left, do U' L' U L U F U' F'. Repeat
for all four middle edges.

## Step 4: the yellow cross

Look at the yellow side on top. Hold it so the yellow stickers form a line across, or an L shape
at the back left, and do F R U R' U' F'. Repeat until the yellow cross appears.

## Step 5: the last layer

Swap the yellow edges until their side colours match the centres, then put the yellow corners
in their places, and finally turn each yellow corner the right way, for example by repeating
R' D' R D on each corner. Do not panic when the lower layers look scrambled during this step:
they come back when every corner is done.
