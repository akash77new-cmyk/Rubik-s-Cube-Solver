# Following a solution on a real cube

How to use the Solution page without losing your place.

## Hold the cube the right way

Before the first move, hold the cube exactly as the Solution page shows: the right colour facing
you and the right colour on top. Keep holding it that way for the whole solution. Turning the
whole cube in your hands changes which side is "front", and every later move goes wrong.

## Step through the moves

Each move has a picture and a sentence like "Turn the right side away from you." Press Next
(or the right arrow key) after each move. Autoplay moves on by itself at a steady pace, and you
can pause it with the space bar.

## Common mistakes

The most common mistakes are turning a back, left or bottom move the wrong way, doing a quarter
turn instead of a half turn, and losing track of which move you just did. Say each move aloud as
you do it. Go slowly at first: accuracy matters more than speed.

## If something goes wrong

If the cube no longer matches the picture, stop. You can undo your last moves by doing them in
reverse, or simply scan the cube again: the app will find a new solution from wherever you are.
