# Scanning your cube with the camera

How to get a clean scan, and what to do when a sticker is read wrong.

## Good light, no glare

Scan in even light, facing a window or under a ceiling light, not with a lamp right behind the
camera. Glare makes a sticker look white or pale yellow, and dim light makes red and orange
look alike. If a sticker is often misread, change the angle a little or use the HSV
Calibration page.

## Holding a side

Hold one side flat towards the camera so that it fills the outline. The scan guide shows which
colour should face the camera and which colour should be on top. Hold still until the side is
captured: in Auto mode it is captured after you hold it still for about a second, and in Manual
mode you press Capture.

## Any order works

Each side is recognised by its centre colour, so you can scan the six sides in any order, and a
side can never be captured twice. A mirrored camera preview is handled automatically. If a side
was held the wrong way up, the app can usually rotate it back by itself.

## When a sticker is read wrong

After the sixth side, the app checks the whole cube. If it is not solvable, the Smart repair
card looks for the sticker or stickers the camera most likely misread and proposes a fix. You
can apply the fix, rescan that side, or repaint a sticker by hand on the Cube Viewer page.
