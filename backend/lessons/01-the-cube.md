# The cube and its pieces

What a 3×3 cube is made of, and why the centres decide each side's colour.

## Three kinds of pieces

A cube has 26 visible pieces. The 6 centres have one sticker each and never move relative to
each other: they only turn in place. The 12 edges have two stickers, and the 8 corners have
three. A sticker never leaves its piece, so an edge always keeps the same two colours and a
corner the same three.

## The centres decide the colours

Because the centres never move, the centre sticker tells you a side's colour. A side with a
white centre is the white side, even when its other stickers are mixed. This is why the app
recognises each scanned side by its centre colour, and why you can scan the sides in any order.

## The colour scheme

On a standard cube, white is opposite yellow, red is opposite orange, and green is opposite
blue. Opposite colours never meet on one piece: there is no red-orange edge and no
white-yellow corner. With white on top and green in front, red is on the right.

## How many positions there are

A cube can be in about 43 quintillion (43 × 10^18) positions. Even so, every position can be
solved in 20 moves or fewer. This number is called God's number, and it was proven in 2010
with a large computer search.
